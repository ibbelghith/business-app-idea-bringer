### Interview guide

1. Tell me about the last time this happened.
2. What did you do, step by step?
3. How often does it happen? What does it cost in time, money, risk, or lost sales?
4. What tools, people, or workarounds do you use now?
5. Have you paid to solve it or tried another solution? What happened?
6. Who decides and controls the budget?

Do not pitch the app during problem interviews. Record participant context, date, direct observations/quotes with permission, and what evidence would disconfirm the hypothesis.
